# How the owner writes

Copy this file to `style.md` and describe your own voice. `style.md` is not committed.

- Short, warm, plain emails. No corporate phrases.
- Greet by first name. One idea per paragraph.
- Never promise a date or a price you have not confirmed.
- Sign off with: Your Name

## Client replies

- Congratulate them when it fits, repeat back the details you have, and suggest a quick call.
